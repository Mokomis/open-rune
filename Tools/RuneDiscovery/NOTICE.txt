Open Rune discovery integration

HyperDHT 6.35.0 is used for public discovery, Noise-authenticated admission and
unordered encrypted messages. Bare Kit 2.5.5 embeds the runtime in Android.
The app never downloads or executes remote JavaScript. Game data and saves are
not part of these dependencies. This private integration is not endorsed by
Holepunch or upstream SurrealEngine.

Our integration adds bounded metadata validation, slot admission/credential
rotation, a loopback UDP adapter, Android lifecycle/UI and a desktop launcher.
Original dependency notices follow in the runtime/ and npm/ subdirectories.
Bare native addons are repackaged with Android library names by bare-link.
The Bare Kit release binary is otherwise unmodified except optional debug-symbol
stripping during APK packaging. The pinned download and generated hashes are
recorded by build_android.py in manifest.json.
