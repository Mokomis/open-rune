# Fire and combat effects — October 27, 2026

**Video** and **Enhancements** share one **Fire & combat effects** setting:
Original / Enhanced. Enhanced is the default. Switching applies immediately;
the global Original comparison also disables it. Diagnostic Android intent
`fireEffects` and desktop `--rune-effects=0|1` override a test session.

## Scope

- Normal `Fire`, `TorchFire`, `SmallFire`, and `BigFire` particles use animated
  flame tongues and a few rising embers. Normal and upright billboard modes
  retain their original orientation.
- The common `RuneFX2.DMflame2` world-surface flame uses a 64-frame, two-second animation
  with embers in the texture itself. Its original UV placement, brush geometry,
  and light sources remain intact. Other procedural textures remain original:
  Rune uses FireTexture for lightning, lava, teleporters and magic too.
- `HitMetal`, `HitWeapon` and `SparkSystemHit` use warm, motion-aligned sparks;
  wood and stone impacts use darker debris; blood and green blood use colored
  droplets aligned with their existing motion.
- Original scripts still decide when/where particles spawn, their velocity,
  lifetime, collisions and damage. Rendering adds no gameplay actors, network
  messages, gameplay random calls, dynamic lights, or full-screen passes.

The same renderer applies in multiplayer to effects already present on that
client. This change does not implement missing multiplayer effect replication.

## Cost and safeguards

Replacement particle quads and extra embers share a 192-quad budget per view.
Beyond the budget or 1,800 world units, particles retain original rendering;
extra embers are limited to nearby fire. Portals have independent view budgets.
The small procedural sprite atlases are built during map loading and reused.
Particle sprites have mip chains. Surface fire retains one mip, like its source.
The initial mip-only correction looked clean at a distance but did not resolve
the close-up artifact: the authored crossed flame planes wrap their UVs through
the texture bottom. The replacement now preserves the original black border
(rows 107–127) instead of repeating its hot base above the flame.

No existing game package, save, or mod asset is rewritten. Sprite artwork is
generated by the renderer's own mathematical functions; no external effect
assets or mod code were introduced.

## Reproduction

`Tools/RuneValidation/android_effects.py` uses isolated saves and session-only
native-resolution / 4x MSAA overrides, with the Qualcomm system driver. It
captures fixed-step Original/Enhanced particle previews, then measures real
presentation in Original / Enhanced / Enhanced / Original order. The combat
case replenishes health and shield durability to keep a fight running; its
damage counters must keep increasing. Fixed-step combat may still diverge and
must not be described as identical pixel workloads.

`--probe-effects` (Android `effectsProbe`, with `saveTest`) is a diagnostic
fixture only: it spawns samples of five impact classes plus TorchFire. It
requires an isolated save directory. `RUNE_EFFECTS_DRAW kind=1..6` confirms
actual enhanced draws, and `RUNE_EFFECTS_WORLD` identifies the world flame.

Local image/log evidence is under `outputs/fire-effects` outside Git. The
headless particle regression checks simulation only, explicitly `rendered=0`;
Android captures and runtime logs provide the separate rendering evidence.

## Close-up fire and animation follow-up

The user reported crossed bright lines above a sconce and excessive animation
speed. `--probe-fire-geometry` confirmed two vertical flame planes whose upper
UVs wrap to approximately row 123. Original DMflame2 is black there. The
procedural replacement now keeps the original flame envelope (base near V=.82)
and the entire bottom border clear. This is a replacement-art placement fix,
not suppression of a second legacy particle effect.

The loop now has 64 frames at 32 frames/second: two seconds instead of one,
without halving its sampling cadence. Rising supplemental embers also move at
half their previous speed. The headless `--rune-probe --probe-effects-art` check
passes all 64 frames' black-border and cycle-duration assertions. Desktop and
ARM builds pass. Device follow-up evidence is recorded separately from the
earlier performance measurements above.

The follow-up APK was installed and its close-up sconce capture visually reviewed
on OPD2515 at native 2520x1680 / 4x MSAA: no detached bright strip. The 64-frame,
two-second cycle is confirmed by the renderer log. No new sustained FPS result
is claimed: battery reached 2% before the final measurement. The previous normal
preferences were restored exactly and the 144 Hz rule remains 4.

It reads the same health and effect actors in single player and multiplayer; no protocol/server update is required. Rendering must have observed the pawn before the hit. Bloodless/green impacts alone do not start a red trail.
